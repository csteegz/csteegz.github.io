When I was younger, I used to go to a frisbee tournament in Wildwood NJ.
This was mostly an excuse to drink on the beach.
The second time I went, I realized that the Daiquiri was easy to adapt to a large format and delicious with Dragonberry Bacardi (hey I was in college).
This proved delicious and dangerous - but everyone was super impressed to be enjoying a cocktail on the beach. 
As I've gone to other events, I've repeated this - some ice, a bottle of Rose's Lime Cordial and some Gin and you have a party's worth of (acceptable while camping) gimlets.

But from a cocktail newsletter I subscribe to, I think I've finally found the best way to prep growler cocktails, as well as the best recipe to enjoy them down. 

This is an adaptation of [Nick Suderman's lemonade recipe](https://cocktailswithsuderman.substack.com/p/when-life-gives-you-lemons-make-blackberry) to be made in a growler to enjoy over a weekend.

## The Colin Thomas

Makes 1 growler, 6 drinks
{: .yield}

### Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient |
|-------:|:-----------|
| 8 oz | lemon juice, freshly squeezed and strained <small>or lime juice if you prefer</small> |
| 1 cup | sugar |
| 10 oz | gin <small>or the liquor of your choice, though try to be inspired by a classic cocktail</small> |
| 8 oz | ice, by weight |
| to taste | flavored bitters |
| 32 oz | cold water |
| 1 pinch | salt |

### Directions

1. Take an insulated growler and add the sugar (use a funnel if necessary), salt and lemon juice.
2. Shake in the growler to combine.
3. Add the ice, gin, bitters and water.
4. Shake in the growler to combine.
5. Add ice, lemon juice or water to taste.
